# Digital Compass
Its mission is to help older adults navigate the digital world safely.

# Why I Started This Project 

This project started with my grandmother.

While I was watching her use technology, I noticed how convincing AI-generated videos had become. Seeing how easily she mistook them for real content made me realize how vulnerable many older adults are to misinformation, deepfakes, and online scams.

Around that same time, I noticed something else while watching people around me: technology doesn't always improve people's lives. I noticed that too much screen time and unhealthy technology habits could affect people's daily lives and relationships.

These experiences inspired me to create Digital Compass, an educational platform designed to help older adults navigate the digital world safely and confidently while also encouraging healthier digital habits.

Instead of focusing only on technical skills, the platform includes digital literacy, critical thinking, and responsible technology use. My goal is to help older adults recognize online risks, make informed decisions, and enjoy the benefits of technology without feeling consumed or unsafe. 

I believe technology should be a tool that improves people's lives not something that confuses, manipulates, or controls them. I care deeply about helping others, so Digital Compass was created with that vision in mind.

## Features
Digital Compass has a character named Juan who guides users through different activities based on situations they might encounter online. These activities include topics like deepfakes, online scams, privacy, and digital well-being, helping users learn how to recognize risks and make better decisions.

## Language
Digital Compass was originally created in Spanish for older adults in my community in Colombia. Later, I added an English translation, so the content is now available in both languages.

## Tecnologies Used
I used HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

## Project Status
Digital Compass is a functional first version, but there's still room to improve. I plan to keep developing it step by step as I learn more about programming. In the future, I'd like to add more educational content and interactive activities to help users continue learning about digital safety and well-being.
